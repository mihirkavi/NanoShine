# NanoShine

NanoShine is a commercialization study for a nanoparticle-based approach to disrupting dental biofilms, with an initial focus on daily oral-care applications.

## Research focus

The project explores product formats such as toothpaste, rinses, and water-flossing solutions, along with dentist and hygienist adoption, consumer positioning, market size, and commercial viability.

## Repository contents

This archive includes customer-discovery interviews, survey responses, market-sizing work, commercialization proposals, financial models, presentations, meeting notes, and background research on dental biofilms.

## Status

NanoShine is a research and commercialization concept. The materials do not represent a clinically validated product or medical advice.
